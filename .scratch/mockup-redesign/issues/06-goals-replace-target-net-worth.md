# Goals replace Target Net Worth?

Type: grilling
State: open
Blocked by: #5

## Question

The mockup has **many Goals** (a name plus a target USD net worth, with no date). Each one's date is *derived*, as the first projected month the target is reached ("Reached / Projected ⟨date⟩ · in N yrs / Beyond the N-year projection"). Today there is exactly one **Target Net Worth** (an amount plus a date, in today's purchasing power), plus the **Required Contribution** solver.

Decide:
- whether Goals replace Target Net Worth, and whether a Goal's target is nominal or in today's dollars (it's ambiguous in the mockup);
- the Required Contribution solver's fate (it presumes a dated target and contributions; see 05);
- the CONTEXT.md entries for Goal, and retiring Target Net Worth and Required Contribution;
